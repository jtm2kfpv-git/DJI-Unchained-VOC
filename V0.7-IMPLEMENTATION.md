## Direct pipeline target

```text
DJI H.264 access units
        │
        ├──► lossless MP4 muxer or raw H.264 (original incoming stream)
        │
        └──► MediaCodec decoder → GPU crop/render
                                  ├──► phone preview
                                  └──► MediaCodec H.264 encoder → MediaMuxer MP4
```

This avoids recording application controls, removes the screen-capture consent path, and makes the 9:16 output independent of the phone display geometry.
